Vidyut Rakshak

Stopping the fire before it starts

Most fire-safety systems react to flame or smoke — by the time they trigger, an electrical fault has already ignited something. Vidyut Rakshak watches the wiring itself, catching the arcing signature of a developing fault and cutting power to that circuit before it ever reaches ignition temperature.

How It Works

Three steps, ahead of ignition

The same rate-of-rise logic Kavach already uses for heat and water, applied to current — catching the fault while it's still just a fault.

01 ⚡

Monitor Current

A non-invasive current transformer clips onto the circuit and continuously reads live current draw — no rewiring required.

→
02 〰

Detect Arc Signature

The waveform is compared against the erratic, high-frequency signature of arcing — distinct from a normal overload or surge.

→
03 ■

Trip Circuit + Alert

Power to that circuit is cut before it reaches ignition temperature, and the fault is reported to the Kavach dashboard — the same way Extingo reports a confirmed fire.
